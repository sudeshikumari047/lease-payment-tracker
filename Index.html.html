Interest rate assumption

Enter the annual interest rate from your finance agreement or lender repayment schedule. This tracker uses a nominal annual rate divided by 12 and a reducing-balance calculation.

Do not guess the rate. The facility amount and instalment alone may not identify the lender's exact interest method, fees or insurance treatment.

Data and security

Records are saved in local browser storage. Use backup regularly. The login credentials are embedded in this HTML and are not secure against someone who can inspect the file. For real protection, use a password-protected device/account and keep backup files private.
